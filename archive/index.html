Tompkins and Cortland counties, New York · 1973–2003

Labor Archive

Papers, newspapers, and photographs made by working people organizing in their own towns. Pick a collection to start reading.

1973–2003 · 213 items · 503 page images

Labor Coalition records

The working papers of the Tompkins-Cortland Labor Coalition, later the Midstate Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO. They run from the first meetings in 1984 through the 1986 Cargill salt mine strike, the campaign for an Ithaca plant-closing law, the investigation of Smith Corona's layoffs, and the council's move into the AFL-CIO.

How it works

Find, read, download, cite

  1. FindSearch words that appear anywhere in the documents, or browse by topic, kind of document, and year.
  2. ReadEvery item opens to its pages in reading order. Zoom in to inspect handwriting, stamps, and signatures.
  3. DownloadEach item comes as a PDF with searchable text and as a plain-text file.
  4. CiteEach item has a permanent link and a ready-made citation you can copy.

The page text is read from the scans (OCR) and contains errors. Always check quotations against the page image.

Care

What this archive leaves out

This archive publishes historical records, not research data. It never includes interview recordings or excerpts, private fieldnotes, internal documents shared by present-day partners, personal contact information, or unpublished maps of relationships between people and organizations.

Within the historical records, pages with home addresses, individual pay, or personnel disputes are held back, and so are newspaper articles still under copyright. If you see something that should not be public, write to ar2539@cornell.edu.
