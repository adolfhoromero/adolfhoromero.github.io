Labor Federation Annual Meeting
On March 8th the delegates of the Central

New York Labor Federation, AFL-CIO met to discuss
upcoming issues and to elect officers to the Federa-
tion’s Executive Board. The delegates were given
updates from each Central Labor Couneil in the Fed
eration as well as an overview of the budget. The
delegates then made phone calls to their elected
state leadership leaving messages about the gover-
nor’s proposed budget. They also wrote letters to
their Congressional Representatives asking them to
vote against proposed union reporting requirements
that would cost unions billions of dollars and would
disclose all union finances to union-busting consult-
ants as well as our employers.

The delegates also discussed how to bring
more resources to the region for organizing new
workers and are sending letters to international un-
ion presidents asking that they devote more re-
sources to organizing workers in our region. The final
act of business at the meeting was to discuss the im-
pending war on Iraq. The delegates voted unani-
mously to endorse the resolution passed by the Ex-
ecutive Council of the AFL-CIO stating its opposition
to the war on Iraq. The minutes of the meeting as
well as a copy of the resolution can be found on the
Federation’s website www.cnylabor.org.
