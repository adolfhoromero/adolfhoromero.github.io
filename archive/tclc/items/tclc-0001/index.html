Rules Governing AFL-CIO Local Central Bodies (pamphlet)

Issued February 26, 1973

AFL-CIO Publication No. 13, the national rules for local central bodies (labor councils), photographed as a booklet from the cover through pages 12–13. The rules cover charters, delegates, officers, strikes, boycotts, and labor publications. The Labor Coalition used these rules when it rewrote its constitution to affiliate with the AFL-CIO.

This 1973 edition was filed with the Coalition's 1993–94 affiliation papers. Photographed back-to-front; shown here in reading order. Pages 12–13 were photographed twice.

23. Publications and Advertising.

Experience has demonstrated that irresponsible
publishers of so-called labor papers, yearbooks
and directories and/or unscrupulous solicitors of
advertising for such publications and for so-called
advertising boards, have, on Occasion, exploited
the name and good reputation of the AFL-CIO
to further their own selfish ends, and to the detri-
ment of the best interests of organized labor.
Therefore, no local central body shall be asso-
ciated in any way with a so-called advertising
board, or with a yearbook or labor directory when

such yearbook or labor directory accepts com-
mercial advertising of any kind.

_ It should be clearly understood that labor pub-
lications should exist for the sole purpose of fur-
thering the best interests of the labor movement
not as a source of additional revenue. Therefore,
advertising should be accepted in labor publica.
