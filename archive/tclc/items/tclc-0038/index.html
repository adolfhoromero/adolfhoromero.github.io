Cargill Strikers Support Committee agenda, August 18, 1986

August 18, 1986

A report from the miners' meeting, picket-line phoning, a coalition Labor Day picnic and rally, a Moosewood benefit, a fact-finding hearing on the salt contracts (September 3), and outreach, including a solidarity event in Minneapolis and work on toxic wastes.
