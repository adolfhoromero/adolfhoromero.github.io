Workers Memorial Day events report, April 1992

April 1992Date inferred

A memorial service and OSHA reform rally in Ithaca on April 25 attended by 75 people and dedicated to the 25 victims of the Hamlet, North Carolina, poultry plant fire; a moment of silence at 2 p.m. on April 28, signaled by the City of Ithaca's fire horns and the Cornell chimes; meetings with the district attorneys of Tompkins, Cortland, and Schuyler counties about prosecuting workplace safety crimes; and Workers Memorial Day proclamations by four local governments.

The year is inferred from the 1991–92 annual report and the Hamlet fire of September 1991.

Moment of Silence

In Ithaca the moment of silence was signaled for the 30,000
area residents by the City's fire horns. Precisely at 2 pm on
April 28 the horns sounded the traditional "Death Bell": the
Box-5-5-5-5 signal normally used for the death of a firefighter.
Also in Ithaca, the Cornell University chimes played "Solidarity

Forever" and "Amazing Grace" in honor of the occasion.

District Attorney Meetings on Workplace Health and Safety Crime

Workers Memorial Day this year was also an occasion on which
the labor movement in Central NY opened a new front in our
campaign for safe jobs. Labor delegations met in separate hour
long meetings with the District Attorneys of Tompkins County,
Cortland County and Schuyler County, all on April 28.

The purpose of these meetings was to discuss the possibility
of local criminal prosecution of employers in cases in which
their negligence and willful violation of safety rules leads to
serious workplace injury or illness.

The meetings served to educate the local prosecutors about the
issue of workplace crime, and about the importance of
prosecution of wrongdoers under the state criminal code. Each
meeting resulted in a commitment by the prosecutors to initiate
investigations and pursue such cases where sufficient evidence
that a worker death or serious injury resulted from negligence
or willful violations was brought to their attention. They also
expressed willingness to continue the dialogue with labor on
this issue. We were encouraged by their response.
