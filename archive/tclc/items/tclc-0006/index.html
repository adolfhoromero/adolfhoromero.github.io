Coert Bonthius to Mary Crayton: the Coalition adopts its new constitution

March 22, 1994

The coordinator reports that on October 6, 1993, more than 40 people from 21 unions met at Tompkins Cortland Community College and adopted a new constitution. The letter lists the participating unions and says the council looks forward to a constructive relationship with the national and regional AFL-CIO.
