“Political Action and Education in 1984”: workshop program

April 14, 1984

A free Saturday workshop held by the Coalition with the Extension Division of Cornell's ILR School, at the ILR Conference Center on April 14, 1984. Sessions cover voter registration, Reagan's record on employment and labor, women's issues, and how to get union members active.

The Tompkins-Cortland Labor Coalition
in cooperation with
The New York State School of Industrial and Labor Relations
Extension Division

Presents a Workshop on

POLITICAL ACTION AND EDUCATION IN 1984

Saturday, April 14, 1984 9:30 AM - 12:00 Noon

ILR Conference Center Room 120 Cornell University Campus

Agenda

9:15 AM Coffee and Donuts

930 i Voter Registration
-Who is registered?
-How to register the unregistered.
-Party enrollment.

10:00 II. Reagan's Record
-Employment and Labor
-Womens' Issues
-Foreign Policy

-- BREAK --
. How to Get Your Point Across
-Listening and talking to members.
-What are the members' concerns?
-How to connect the members' concerns and labor's program.

How Can We Work Together?

Adjourn

This Program is FREE OF CHARGE!
