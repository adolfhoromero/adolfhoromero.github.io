General meeting notice, March 26 [1997], with proposed constitutional amendments

March 26, [1997]Date inferred

A blue sheet announcing a general meeting at TC3, where Al Davidoff showed “In the Shadow of the Tower,” about Cornell service and maintenance workers' living wage struggle. The reverse proposes amendments to Article VII (Executive Board) and Article X (meeting frequency).

GENERAL MEETING NOTICE

Date: Wednesday, March 26

Time: 7:30
Place: Tompkins Cortland Community College, The

Forum (TC3 is off Route 13 in Dryden. The
Forum is right off the main entrance)

SPECIAL EVENT

Al Davidoff, President of UAW Local 2300, will
show and discuss the film, "In the Shadow of the
Tower," about the plight of service and mainte-
nance workers at Cornell University, and talk

about their current contract struggle for a liv-
able wage.

BUSINESS ITEMS

1. Proposed Constitutional amendments

(see other
Side for proposed change) .

2. Further discussion of the AFL-CIO’s new program
for Central Labor Councils.

3. The Labor Party’s livable wage campaign.

4. Local union news
