Agenda for the June 5, 1984 meeting at TC3

June 5, 1984

Speaker Tony Gaenslen, approval of the steering committee, an organizing drive, negotiation updates, and volunteers for committees, including a Labor Day picnic, a union directory, the Ithaca Festival, fundraising, and health and safety.
