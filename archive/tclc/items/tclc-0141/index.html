Minutes of the April 5 meeting, Tompkins-Cortland Joint Labor Coalition

April 5, [1984]Date inferred

Acting chair Susan Schlaepfer restates the coalition's objectives, members debate whether non-union members of the labor community such as the Unemployed Councils can join, and the proposed structure is approved. The minutes also cover meeting dates and plans for a political action workshop.

The year is inferred from the neighboring 1984 agendas and minutes.

Dues for the organization were proposed at an initial assessment of five
dollars per union, with this fee subject to change as need arises. Mail-
ings were to be made for each meeting, with each union responsible for

notifying its membership.

Jonathan Rosenblum, of the Ithaca Journal Employees Union, asked for
support from the coalition for his union in their contract troubles, in the
form of postcard mailings, telephone calls, cancelled subscriptions, etc.,
to bring public opinion to bear on their problems. Members present decided
to bring this problem to the attention of their memberships, and to send a

fact sheet from the Ithaca Journal Employees Union along with the next mailing.
