Letter, 1 page

Sheriff Robert Howard's reply on policing the Cargill strike

August 1, 1986

The Tompkins County sheriff replies that deputies at Cargill are there to protect everyone involved and will not take sides. He warns that loaded trucks cannot easily stop and that anyone who violates the law will be arrested.
