Press release: “Union Leader Takes New Position” (Al Davidoff)

March 25, 1997

Announces that Al Davidoff, president of UAW Local 2300 and co-chair of the Labor Coalition, will become the AFL-CIO's Director for New York State. It recounts his path from Cornell custodian to union founder and his role in founding the Coalition in 1984. Two copies were photographed.

For Immediate Release
March 25, 1997

Union Leader Takes New Position

Al Davidoff, President of UAW Local 2300 and Co-chair
ef the Labor Coalition, today announced that as of Monday
March 31 he will be leaving these positions to become the
AFL-CIO’s Director for New York State.

Davidoff attended the ILR School at Cormeld from 1976-
1980 during which time he began working full-time as a
custodian. He became active in the effort to organize among
Cornell workers which culminated in the successful union

vote in 1981 that created Local 2300.

Davidoff was elected the first President of UAW 2300 in
1982. In 1984, he was instrumental in the founding of the
Labor Coalition, bringing together 40 unions from the Ithaca

and Cortland area and has served as that organization’s
Chair since then.

Over the past decade, Davidoff helped over a dozen

other workplaces unionize, including HOMES, Video Ithaca,
the Holiday Inn, and the Public Library.

Davidoff is perhaps best known for forcefully bringing
local labor struggles into the public realm in campaigns

new aggressive campaign to mobilize the Labor movement
around organizing, rank-and-file activism, political action

and the fight for all working families in our communities.
He will remain an Ithaca resident.
