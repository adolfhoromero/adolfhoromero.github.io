Model letter to Cargill salt customers: “It is rarely a ‘freak’ accident…”

[1986]Date inferred

A draft letter for supporters to send to municipalities and other buyers of road salt that had received bids from Cargill. It argues that workplace deaths are rarely freak accidents and asks buyers to consider Cargill's labor and safety record.
