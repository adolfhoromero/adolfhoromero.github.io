Summary of conference and training activities, first quarter 1991–92

August–September 1991

The state's quarterly reporting form, listing six training sessions held from August 1 to September 23, 1991, on a clean-room chemical hazard, health and safety committees for UAW Locals 1774 and 1326, and health and safety surveys for CSEA Local 812, with the number of participants planned and attending.
