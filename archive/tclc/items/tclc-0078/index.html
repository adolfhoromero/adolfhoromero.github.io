GENERAL MEETING NOTICE

Date: Wednesday, June 11
Time: PRS)

Place: Tompkins Cortland Community College, The
Forum (TC3 is off Route 13 in Dryden. The
Forum is right off the main entrance)

Special Event:
Dan Addy, President of Local 961, Inter-
national Brotherhood of Electrical Work-
ers, will show and discuss the film,
"Power Switch," which exposes the reality
behind efforts to deregulate the utility
industry.

Business Items:
1. Steering Committee nominations and
election.

2. Discussion of proposal to make the
Working Press more of a regional Union

paper.

3. Health and Safety training grant
plans.

4. Union reports.

LABOR ALERT!

A Central NY delegation will join thousands in
Detroit on June 21 for Action! Motown ‘97, a mass
mobilization in solidarity with 2,000 newspaper
workers who have been fighting the union busters
Gannett and Knight-Ridder for almost two years.
Tacit pets mourerichtemmcall 277-5670, for in-
formation or to reserve space.
