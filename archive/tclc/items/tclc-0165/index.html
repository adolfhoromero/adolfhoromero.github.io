TOMPKINS CORTLAND LABOR COALITION

701 W. State STREET
ItHAcA, NY 14850

bear Brothers and Sisters:

Please pass this letter to the appropriate officer(s) of your union, for
it is time to renew your union's membership in the Tompkins Cortland Labor

Coalition.

In December 1984, a new dues structure was approved by the membership of
the Tompkins Cortland Labor Coalition. The new dues structure is as follows:

*2 to 49 members in your union - $30 per year, not to exceed one dollar
per member

*50 to 100 members in your union - $60 per year

*T0I or more members in your union - $100 per year

Based on the membership of your union, please remit your check to Jim Nichols,
127 Snyder Hill Road, Ithaca NY 14850. Please make checks payable to Tompkins

Cortland Labor Coalition.

The following unions have renewed their memberships for 1985. Carpenters
Local # 603; Cornell Security Employees Union; CSEA Local # 855; IBEW Local # 241;
Ithaca City School District Employees Association; Itiaca Police Benevolent
Association; Ithaca Teachers Association; Painters Local # 178; Plumbers &
Pipefitters Local # 109; UAW Local # 2300; and United University Professions.

The next General Membership Meeting of the Tompkins Cortland Labor Coalition
is Tuesday, June IIth, at 7PM. The meeting will be held at the Plumbers & Pipe-
fitters Hall, 70I West State Street, Ithaca. Please attend the next meeting and
offer your suggestions and assistance for the LABOR DAY RALLY 1985 in September.

If you have any questions, please contact: Jim Morrisette, Chair of the
Coalition, 272-8314; Al Davidoff, Co-Chair of the Coalition, 272-4108; Jim
Nichols, Treasurer of the Coalition, 844-8211.

Respectfully,

Vwi

Jim Nichols
Treasurer
