HEY YOU UNION PEOPLE

e@eeceeoaanes

* Do you ever feel lonely?

* Do you ever wish you had a friend in high places? "]

Does the world sometimes feel like one big Personnel
Office?

* Want to feel like a loyal, decent, normal worker
and citizen again?

eceeeeee then Come hear friend of Labor and Mayor of Ithaca
John Gutenberger speaking at the August meeting of
the Tompkins-Cortland Joint Labor Coalition.

Thursday, August 2, 1984 at 1960 hours at the Plumbers
and Pipefitters Hall 701 West State Street in Ithaca
