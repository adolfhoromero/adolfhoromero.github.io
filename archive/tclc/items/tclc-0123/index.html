Agreement, 2 pages

Joint resolution between the Steuben County and Midstate central labor councils

1996

A draft agreement for joint work: expanding The Working Press to Steuben, combining political action across congressional and state districts, solidarity support, extending Midstate's health and safety program, and exploring a shared staff position. It plans a joint meeting in January 1997.

JOINT RESOLUTION
between
The Steuben County Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO
and

The Midstate Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO

This Joint Resolution is entered into this Gay of

1996, between the Steuben County Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO,
and, The Midstate Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO.

Bie

i The Steuben County Central Labor Council, and the
Midstate Central Labor Council agree to enter into

joint work on specific programs and projects. These
programs and projects include:

a. The Working Press. Expand news coverage and distribution
to include unions affiliated with the Steuben County Central

Labor Council and events occurring within the Steuben County
Central Labor Council’s jurisdiction.

b. Political Action. Combine efforts to facilitate a greater
level of political involvement from union members. Specifi-
cally, combine efforts in the electoral arena on the basis
that Congressional, NYS Assembly, and NYS Senate districts
cross both labor council’s jurisdictions.

c. Solidarity Work. Combine efforts of both labor councils
for the support of each labor council’s affiliates.

dad. Health and Safety. Expand the Midstate Central Labor
Council’s occupational health and safety program to include
the Steuben County Central Labor Council and its affiliates.

Both labor councils agree to explore the feasibility of
jointly financing one full-time staff position to be shared
between the two labor councils for the purpose of organizing

and implementing programs and projects jointly agreed to by
both labor councils.

Both labor councils agree to a procedural format where pro-
gramming and project will be discussed first and financing
of a shared staff position will be discussed secondarily.
