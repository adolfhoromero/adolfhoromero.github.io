“The strike against Cargill is still going!!” flyer on city and county salt contracts

[Summer–Fall 1986]Date inferred

A Cargill Strikers Solidarity Committee flyer asking people to tell city and county representatives to delay salt-contract bidding. It lists cases against Cargill, including mine-safety violations and a race- and sex-discrimination settlement, and asks people to sign the committee's petition.

Arbitrations concluding that Cargill Salt
fired workers without ,just cause

While under the direction of Mine Manager Gerry
Thornton, Cargill Salt has resorted at least twice to
discharge of employees in cases where only lesser
disciplinary action was warranted under the terms of
the contract with the I.A.M. Specifically, in cases
resolved July 29, 1985 (FMSC #85K/08236) and

November 26, 1985 (FMCS #85K16936), arbitrators
ruled that two workers were discharged without just

cause. Copies of the arbitrators’ rulings are available
from I.A.M., 638 Elmira Road, Ithaca, NY 14850.
