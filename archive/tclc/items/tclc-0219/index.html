LAW OFFICES
BAKER, CLARK, SATTER & DALY

608 STATE TOWER BUILDING
SYRACUSE, NEW YORK 13202

TELEPHONE: (315) 471-3027

JAMES M. BAKER

MIMIC, SATTER*
MICHAEL P. DAL May 1, 1990
*Aiso admitted to D.C. Bar

Coert Bonthius, Coordinator
Tompkins/Cortland Labor Coalition

109 West State Street
Ithaca, New York 14850

Dear Coert:

Late last week I spoke with Fred Fienstein, an
attorney working with one of the labor committees on Capitol
Hill. He was quite involved in drafting the WARN act. I
believe he will be helpful in resolving the "operating unit”
issue. I suggest you call him at 202-225-5768. If you
mention my name, he should know who you are.

I look forward to speaking with you after you and
Fred have spoken.

Very truly yours,

BAKER, CLARK, SATTER & DALY

Mimi C. Satter
