Resolution of the Board of Supervisors of the City of Cortland on Smith Corona

[1992]Date inferred

A resolution denouncing Smith Corona's decision to move operations from Cortland to Mexico, supporting its workers, and asking the company for help for the community. The coalition submitted it for its Cortland County members.
