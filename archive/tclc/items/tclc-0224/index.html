Press conference agenda: “Smith Corona Runaway, But the Law Can't Touch Them”

May 8, 1991

The order of speakers for the coalition's May 8, 1991 press conference: Coert Bonthius, Carol Kates of UAW 2300 on the research, personal stories from Smith Corona workers, lawyers, and statements from Julie Hurwitz and Frank Barbaro.
