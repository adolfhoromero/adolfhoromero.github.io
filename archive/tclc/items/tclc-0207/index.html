Sugar Law Center news release opposing the Smith Corona layoffs

February 1, 1991

The Maurice and Jane Sugar Law Center for Economic and Social Justice, a project of the National Lawyers Guild in Detroit, announces its opposition to the layoffs and its support for the coalition's investigation.
