The Working Press, Volume 1, Issue 6, June 1998: “Building the Road to Union Cities”

June 1998

This issue explains the AFL-CIO Union Cities program step by step, as the Labor Coalition had endorsed it in 1997. It includes a Local News Alert on organizing locals and contracts, health and safety news, the defeat of California's Proposition 226, and the 1998 meeting schedule. The pages were photographed back-to-front and are shown in reading order.

The Labor Coalition, Midstate Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO

Labor Coalition
Endorsement of AFL-

ClO program
In 1997, The Labor Coalition endorsed the
AFL-CIO Union City initiative. It is the hope
that each and every Local Union in Tompkins,
Cortland and Schuyler counties will adopt the
Union City initiative. Together, we can
BUILD A UNION CITY.

Step 1. ORGANIZING FOR
CHANGE, CHANGING TO
ORGANIZE
Promote organizing as the entire labor
movement's top priority and get half of the local
union to sign up for the AFL-CIO “Changing to

Organize” Program.

Step 2: MOBILIZING AGAINST
ANTI-UNION EMPLOYERS
Recruit and activate at least 1% (one percent)
of union members in the area for STREET
HEAT, AFL-CiO’s new solidarity and rapid
response team. Members pledge to help
defend and support workers involved in

organizing and contract campaigns.

Are YOU ready to commit to
STREET HEAT?

Step 3. BUILDING POLITICAL
POWER AND COMMUNITY
COALITIONS
Organize grassroots lobbying/political
action committees fo work on local, state
and national issues, build community
alliances and support political candidates

who champion working families.

Step 4. PROMOTING
ECONOMIC GROWTH,
PROTECTING OUR
COMMUNITIES
Organize community allies in support of
economic development strategies that
establish worker and family-friendly
community standards for local industries

and public investment.

Step 5. EDUCATING MEMBERS
IN POCKETBOOK ECONOMICS
Sponsor the new AFL-CIO Economics
Education program for at least a majority
of local unions in the area.

Step 6. GENERATING
SUPPORT FOR THE RIGHT TO
ORGANIZE
Persuade city or town council and/or
county bodies to pass resolutions
supporting the right of workers to organize
and insist candidates do likewise as a

condition of endorsement.

Step 7. MAKING SURE OUR
LEADERSHIP MIRRORS FACES
OF OUR MEMBERS
Work to make sure that all official CLC
bodies - executive board, committees,
Officers, delegate bodies-are as diverse as
the membership represented by affiliated

focal unions.

LABOR IS BACK - THE DEFEAT OF PROP

DEFEAT OF THE PAYCHECK DECEPTION ACT IN
CALIFORNIA - A MAJOR VICTORY FOR LABOR

Dr. Michael Lax, Medical Director of
the CNY Occupational Health Clini-
cal Center (CNYOHCC) will con-
duct a health screening for repetitive
strain injury. Also available will be
Greg Siwiniski, Certified Industrial
Hygienist with the CNYOHCC, who

on workstations; and Arlene Small,
Physical Therapist, who will consult
with clinic participants on helpful
stretches.

The following is a memo from AFL-CIO President John J. Sweeney to all
Labor Activists...."A moder political miracle, the defeat of Califomia Propo-
sition 226 by an overall 54 to 46% margin, sprang from the voters strong
sense of fairness and union members’ unprecedented mobilization against
a right-wing effort to silence the voices of working men and women in our
nation’s political system. Union members rejected the proposition with a
resounding 71 to 29 percent vote. Even more, we gained new strength and
momentum for a pro-working family agenda heading into the fall elections.
More than 24,000 new union member political activists emerged in Califor-

nia in the course of this campaigning.

Anyone who is concerned that their
job may be putting them at risk for
carpal tunnel syndrome or other hand/
wrist/shoulder problems, or thinks
they have symptoms of such an injury,
is welcome to come in for a free con-
sultation. Refreshments and general
information on RSI will also be avail-
able.

The defeat of Proposition 226 sends a clear message about the prospects of
other such worker-bashing initiatives in other state and the message is,
simply put, “Pounding working families is a losing proposition.”...

For nearly six months, we've played effective defense against the attempt
to wipe us off the political map. Now it’s time to go on the offensive on
behalf of a Working Family Agenda, demanding that elected officials take
action in support of the right to organize and on behalf of the key worker
concerns like health care, fair pay, and retirement security.”

If you would like to join the Labor Coalition in our efforts to promote a
working family agenda during this political season, contact Kathy
Thomas at 257-9586.
