“Repetitive Strain Injury: A Free Clinic” flyer, Bath, New York

Friday, October 16, [1998]Date inferred

A free clinic on Friday, October 16, at American Flint Glass Workers Local 1013 in Bath, with Dr. Michael Lax of the CNY Occupational Health Clinical Center, an industrial hygienist, an ergonomist, and a physical therapist. It was co-sponsored by the Midstate and Steuben central labor councils and AFGWU Local 1013.

The year is inferred: October 16 fell on a Friday in 1998, and the June 1998 Working Press announces an earlier clinic at the same union hall. The staff ergonomist named here was announced in September 1998.

REPETITIVE STRAIN INJURY:
A FREE CLINIC

Date: Friday October 16
Time: 1PM-4 PM
Clinician: Dr. Michael Lax

2-4 Pultney Square West, Bath, NY
American Flint Glass Workers Local 1013

Dr. Lax, Medical Director of the CNY Occupational Health Clinical Center (CNYOHCC),
will conduct a health screening for repetitive strain injury. Also available will be Greg
Siwinski, Certified Industrial Hygienist, and Greg Shaw, Ergonomist, who will conduct
individual consultations on workstations, and Arlene Small, Physical Therapist, who will
~ consult with clinic participants on helpful stretches. Anyone who is concerned that their job
may be putting them at risk for carpal tunnel syndrome or other hand/wrist/elbow/shoulder
problems, or thinks they have symptoms of such an injury, is welcome to come in for a free
consultation. Refreshments and general information on RSI will also be available.

SPONSORED BY MIDSTATE CENTRAL LABOR COUNCIL
STEUBEN CENTRAL LABOR COUNCIL
AMERICAN FLINT GLASS WORKERS LOCAL 1013

FUNDED IN PART BY NYS DEPARTMENT OF LABOR
