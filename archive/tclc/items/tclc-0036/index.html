Cargill Strikers Support Committee: report on union locals outreach

August 15, 1986

A list of the locals that had sent members to the Cargill picket line (Painters Local 178, UAW Local 2300, IAM, St. Joe's miners [USWA], Cornell faculty, Ithaca Teachers Association, and others), plus speaking engagements and letters sent to other unions. Two copies were photographed.
