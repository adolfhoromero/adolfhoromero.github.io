FYI: A Health and Safety Newsletter for Office Workers, September 1991

September 1991

The Coalition's newsletter for office workers. This issue reports an RSI epidemic at SUNY Cortland: in a CSEA Local 605 survey of clerical and secretarial staff, almost two-thirds of those who responded had at least one warning symptom of repetitive strain injury.
