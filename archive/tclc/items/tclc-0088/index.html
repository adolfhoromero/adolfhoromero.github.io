LABOR ALERT on the TEAM Act (S. 295), with “clone sheep” flyer

March 18, 1997

An urgent letter to solidarity activists asking them to call Senator D'Amato to oppose the TEAM Act, which labor argued would legalize company unions. The reverse is an AFL-CIO flyer: “Science has discovered the ability to clone sheep. Now Big Business wants to ‘clone’ itself into company unions.”

LABOR ALERT

THE LABOR COALITION

Midstate Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO

(607) 277-5670 1-800-804-8252 Fax (607) 272-4111

March 18, 1997

Dear Labor Solidarity Activist,

The Senate will shortly be considering the
TEAM Act (S.295), and SENATOR D’AMATO NEEDS TO
KNOW THAT WORKING FAMILIES IN TOMPKINS, CORTLAND
AND SCHUYLER COUNTIES OPPOSE THIS AND WILL HOLD
THEIR LAWMAKERS ACCOUNTABLE. CALL HIM TODAY (1-
800/522-6721) AND TELL HIM TO VOTE NO ON S.295.
(More information on reverse of this sheet -
please post the flyer.)

The TEAM Act is dangerous because it would
allow employers to hand-pick who would represent
workers on "employee committees" that deal with
wages, hours, and other terms and conditions of
employment. This would allow management to dic-
tate and control who would speak on behalf of
workers and what subjects could be discussed. It
would also give unscrupulous employers a powerful
tool to undermine organizing drives. In effect it

would legalize the establishment of company un-
ions.

The TEAM Act would overturn one of the basic
principles of our labor law - that workers should
have the right to self-determination by democrati-

Cally electing their own independent representa-
tives.

This call for action is only being sent lo-

cally to a small group of Solidarity Activists.

We are counting on each and Every one of you to
respond. Thanks. 2

In Solidarity,

V (A
Carl Feuer

Interim Coordinator

Science has discovered
the ability to clone sheep.

Now Big Business wants to
‘clone’ itself into company unions, and
the anti-worker forces in Congress
want to let them do it!

SWE have now discovered how to clone an identical sheep from an existing one. Many people
are talking about its potential future effects. For American workers, however, a different type of

“cloning” is being pushed by Big Business and the anti-union forces in Congress. It would have
disastrous effects on workers’ ability to have an independent voice in the workplace.

The U.S. Senate is set to vote this week on legislation known as the TEAM Act (S.295). Under this
bill, management would be given the right to dominate and interfere with employee involvement
programs, or “teams.” Under S. 295, management would be allowed to create and disband worker
committees, handpick the committee members (“cloning” management's viewpoint), dictate the
agenda, and even determine the outcome of any decisions made by the committee.

In short, S.295 would give Big Business the ability to establish “company unions!’ The AFL-CIO and
its affiliated unions are strongly opposed to this legislation.

Union members nationwide are being asked to call their U.S. Senators immediately to urge them to
vote NO on this bill when it comes up for a vote.

Simply call the AFL-CIO toll-free action hotline at 1-800-LABOR-21
(1-800-522-6721) and ask to speak to Senator Alfonse D’Amato.
Tell him to vote NO on S. 295.
