Tuesday, October 9, [1984]Date inferred

Speaker Jean McPheeters of the Democratic Committee, negotiation updates including unfair labor practice hearings at Clever Hans Bakery and the Machinists' strike at Emerson Electric, political action, and a proposal for a new dues structure.
