As for the issues which were supposedly dealt with, labor
and the environment, the side agreements are meaningless. In the
new agreements two commissions are to be set up on labor and
environmental standards. According to the President these
commissions will have the power to resolve any concerns Americans
may have about losing jobs to Mexico because of the cheaper
labor, fewer labor rights and poor environmental protections. We

strongly disagree.
First, these commissions may only investigate violations of
law already existing within each country. This will do nothing to
bring Mexico up to U.S. environmental standards.

Second, even if violations are discovered a 2/3 vote of the

commission is needed before a review can be initiated.
Third, there are no timelines outlined for these reviews.

Fourth, these commissions have no enforcement powers to

force violating countries to change their ways.

We continue see the NAFTA as a very bad deal for URS,
Mexican and Canadian workers and communities. The side
agreements announced late last week will only hasten plant
closings such as the one experienced in Cortland last year when
Smith Corona moved to Mexico. NAFTA will drag wages and living
standards in this country further and further towards those of
Mexico and only a very few will benefit.

Some of our Congressional representatives have postponed
announcing their positions on NAFTA until after the side
agreements were completed. We now call upon Representatives
Boehlert and McHugh to come out in opposition to this legislation
and we call upon Representatives Walsh and Hinchey to reaffirm in
the clearest terms possible that they stand in opposition to

NAFTA.
