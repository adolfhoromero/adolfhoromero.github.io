TOMPKINS COUNTY
PUBLIC LIBRARY

To: Support Staff Association/UA W_2300

From: Janet Steiner, Library Director —)—

Re: Voluntary Removal of two confidential positions
Date: April 14, 1997

At the request of the Tompkins County Public Library Board of Trustees, | am seeking
voluntary removal of two confidential positions from the UAW 2300: Stenographic

Secretary and Principal Account Clerk Typist.

Both of these positions are involved in various areas of personnel administration, payroll
processing, labor and vendor contract negotiations, and support functions to the Library
Director, the Assistant Library Director, the Board of Trustees and the TCPL Foundation
Board of Directors. Their responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

e preparation of contract negotiation proposals and related documents

© preparation of payroll
processing of workers compensation, retirement, health insurance claims (including

dependent care demographic information)
support to the Board of Trustees in preparing documents, taking minutes, and

attending executive sessions of the trustees

¢ reviewing employee medical files
e checking references for possible employment, access to personnel files

It is clear that the Fair Employment Act offers this exemption to employees who assist
and act in a confidential capacity to managerial employees. As of this date, we do not
consider any other positions to be considered confidential.
