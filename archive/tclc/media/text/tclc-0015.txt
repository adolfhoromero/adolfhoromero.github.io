September 15, 1993

Leonard Lewis
AFL-CIO

211 E. 43rd, Suite 304
New York, NY 10017

Dear Leonard,

Enclosed you will find a copy, as promised, of the
constitution as ammended after our meeting last month. As per
our phone conversation, our Steering Committee is meeting on
Monday, September 20, between 11:30 and 2:30 to go over this
final draft. If you have any problems or concerns about this
final draft, you should let me know immediately. And, as we
agreed, I will call you at the Sheraton Inn in Liverpool, NY if I
need to contact you between 11:30 and 2:30 on Monday.

If we don’t talk, that is, if you don’t call us with
problems, and we don't call you with problems, you can assume
that this is the constitution which we will be ratifying on
October 6th at our General Meeting. We will assume that this is

I hope it all is. We are looking very much forward to the
affiliation and working with you all in the future. Take care
and I'll be in touch.

In Solidarity,

Coert Bonthius, Coordinator
