Dear Mr. Bonthius:

Per your conversation with my secretary, Eneida Curtis, enclosed find
an Application for Charter, to be filled out and signed by officers of the
newly formed Mid-State Central Labor Council.

Please fill out the form as soon as possible and return to me at the
above address. Your check in the amount of $50 has already been forwarded to
the national AFL-CIO to cover the charter fee.

If I can be of further assistance, please let me know.

In solidarity,
