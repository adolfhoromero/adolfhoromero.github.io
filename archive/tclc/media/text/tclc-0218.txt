Dear Mr. Rappleyea,

Enclosed please find a copy of a letter which has been sent
to Richard Tupper, Chair of the Cortland County Legislature
regarding the layoff of workers at Smith Corona. At this time,
this is just for your information. However, our organization may
be contacting you in the near future about our concerns that Smith
Corona may have violated the federal plant closing law.

In any case, the situation at Smith Corona points up the need
for stronger plant closing and mass layoff legislation in New York

State. The vast majority of workers were given no notification
about their layoffs and those that did get notification had only
a few days to prepare. The right to be informed about your

employment prospects seems only fair.
We hope you will contact us if you have any questions
regarding our informal investigation.

Sincerely,

Coert Bonthius

Dear Marty,

Enclosed please find a copy of a letter which has been sent
to Richard Tupper, Chair of the Cortland County Legislature
regarding the layoff of workers at Smith Corona. At this time,
this is just for your information. However, we may be contacting
you in the near future about our concerns that Smith Corona may
have violated the federal plant closing law. You should know that
even though Smith Corona is in Cortland County a huge number of the
laid off workers come from Tompkins County towns like Groton,
McLean and Freeville.

In any case, the situation at Smith Corona points up the need
for stronger plant closing and mass layoff legislation in New York
State. The vast majority of workers were given no notification
about their layoffs and those that did get notification had only
a few days to prepare. The right to be informed about your
employment prospects seems only fair.

I have been in touch with Frank Barbaro's office already. He
is interested in helping us investigate the situation. It clearly
can be used as support for stronger New York State legislation.

Please contact us if you have any questions regarding our
informal investigation.

Sincerely,

Coert Bonthius
Coordinator
