THE TOMPKINS-CORTLAND LABOR COALITION

The next meeting of the Labor Coalition will be on April 25
at 6:30 PM at 701 West State Street in Ithaca. The meeting will
be followed at 7:30 by the Labor Movie of the Month.

Issues to ve discussed at the meeting include;
1. Final stages of Plant Closing Legislation
2. High School Labor Workshop
3. The Labor Coalition, The League of Women Voters anc
Ecology Action team up to take on NYSEG
4. Nominations for new officers
5. Should the Labor Coalition meet six times a year?
