the Cornell campus, is a small print shop.
Day in, day out, Lew Relyea, Bob Sawchuk
and Tim Cook toil away printing, copying,
binding, cutting and collating. They work

—~—-Yerrtitation in the print shop is extremely

just gets recirculated within the print shop.
Solvents are hazardous to workers’
health. Organic solvents give off invisible
vapors which are dangerous if breathed too
long or in too great an amount. Typical short
term effects are: headache, dizziness,
giddiness, mental confusion, blurred vision,
weakness and even loss of consciousness,

But Local 2300’s Health and Safety
Committee was not satisfied. They knew
that OSHA standards can never be relied on
to protect workers’ health. Many were set
years ago, often with management and
manufacturers influencing the decisions.

recommended a lower limit, 60 parts per

solvent overexposure. :
