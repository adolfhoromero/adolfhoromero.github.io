March 1, 1986

Dear Sisters and Brothers,

Enclosed are notes from our recent special meeting at
Cornell's ILR Extension. As you will notice a number of
important discussions occurred and good suggestions were
made, We hope that our coalition will be able to act on
these ideas for our mutual benefit.

Qur next general meeting is Tuesday, March 11, 7pm at the
Plumbers & Pipefitters Hall in Ithaca. We have a guest
speaker: Brian Kane of the Binghamton Community & Labor
Coalition. He will talk about the success that they have
had in working together on various issues.

This meeting will also present nominations for the 1986
steering committee ~ to be elected in April. Please
consider who you would like to be on the committee. If
you can't attend the meeting you can phone in nominations
to myself at 272-0506 or to Al Davidoff at the UAW office

272-4108, -4111.

We will also be hearing reports and doing some planning on
our various projects (plant closings bill, labor newspaper,
and others).

In Solidarity,

Torn Joyce

Tom Joyce for
the Steering Committee
