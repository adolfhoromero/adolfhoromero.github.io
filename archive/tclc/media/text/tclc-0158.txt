THE TOMPKINS-CORTLAND JOINT LABOR COALITION

An Invitation to City of Ithaca Representatives and County of
Tompkins Representatives and Local Labor Leaders.....

The Tompkins-Cortland Joint Labor Coalition, which represents
twenty-six unions with over 7000 members, invites you to attend
an informational meeting and informal get-together at 701 west
State Street, Ithaca at 7:30 P.M. on Novemcer 15, 1984.

We believe it is vital that elected officials, from all political

parties and persuasions, have the opportunity to sit cown with local

labor leaders in a relaxed atmosphere to share views and information.

Agenda: 1. Introduction to tne Labor Coalition
2. Views and suggestions of labor

3. Views and suggestions of City and County

some subjects which are almost certain to come up are: current

negotiations between the Ithaca Teachers Asso. and the School Ecara,

workers grievances at Clever Hans Hakery, the recent settlement

between the Machinist Union and Emerson Flectronics,

and labor
relations at Cornell University.

Coffee and cookies will be served.

Jim Morrissette, Chair TCZIC
