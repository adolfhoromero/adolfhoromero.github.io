The following is a memo from AFL-CIO President John J. Sweeney to all
Labor Activists...."A moder political miracle, the defeat of Califomia Propo-
sition 226 by an overall 54 to 46% margin, sprang from the voters strong
sense of fairness and union members’ unprecedented mobilization against
a right-wing effort to silence the voices of working men and women in our
nation’s political system. Union members rejected the proposition with a
resounding 71 to 29 percent vote. Even more, we gained new strength and
momentum for a pro-working family agenda heading into the fall elections.
More than 24,000 new union member political activists emerged in Califor-

nia in the course of this campaigning.

Anyone who is concerned that their
job may be putting them at risk for
carpal tunnel syndrome or other hand/
wrist/shoulder problems, or thinks
they have symptoms of such an injury,
is welcome to come in for a free con-
sultation. Refreshments and general
information on RSI will also be avail-
able.

The defeat of Proposition 226 sends a clear message about the prospects of
other such worker-bashing initiatives in other state and the message is,
simply put, “Pounding working families is a losing proposition.”...

For nearly six months, we've played effective defense against the attempt
to wipe us off the political map. Now it’s time to go on the offensive on
behalf of a Working Family Agenda, demanding that elected officials take
action in support of the right to organize and on behalf of the key worker
concerns like health care, fair pay, and retirement security.”

If you would like to join the Labor Coalition in our efforts to promote a
working family agenda during this political season, contact Kathy
Thomas at 257-9586.
