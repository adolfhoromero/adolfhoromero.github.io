B. Program Design/ Implementation:

GOAL # 1:

To educate workers in Tompkins and Cortland counties about occupational hazards

associated with asbestos and hazardous cleaning/maintenance substances used in
custodial and housekeeping occupations.

Objective # 1: To hold at least one 2 to 4 hour training workshop on both

of the above stated hazards with 15 to 30 workers from occupations effected
by these specific problems. Technical expertise for these training workshops
will be obtained from organizations such as Cornell University's Chemical

Hazard Information Program (CHIP) and/or the Central New York Council on
Occupational Safety and Health (CNYCOSH).

Objective # 2: To aggressively work with at least five (5) different unions

(see Objective # 5) to obtain their membership's participation and attendance
at the workshops listed in Objective #1. To have three (3) or more trainees
from each union participate in the workshops.

Objective # 3: At the completion of each training workshop, the Training
Coordinator will assist trainees from each union to present the informa-
tion they have learned to the membership of their local unions.

Objective # 4: In conjunction with participating unions, to produce an
easy-to-read brochure about specific health and safety hazards which
should be of concern to their members. The brochure will be oriented to-
ward problems experienced by workers at specific worksites.

Objective # 5: To raise the level of health and safety awareness among
union members by assisting local unions to develop a plan for the dissemi-
nation of training materials listed in Objective #

Objective # 6: To work with unions, including the following, to train
their members on the following hazards:

A. Asbestos and its' potential hazards.

To assist 250 dairy workers represented by Teamsters Local # 65, to identif

the hazards associated with their occupations; and, to increase labor and man-

Objective # 1: To conduct at least two 2 hour group interviews with dairy
workers from different worksites, so as to identify health and safety pro-
blems associated with their employment. The intervies will focus on working

Objective # 2: To confer with experts, from organizations such as CHIP
and/or CNYCOSH, so as to evaluate employees' perceptions of health and

safety hazards at their job sites and to determine the extent of the risks
faced by the employees.

Objective # 3: To conduct at least two 2 to 4 hour training workshops with
10 to 20 dairy workers who have been interviewed. At least 2 workers from
each dairy represented by Teamsters Local # 65 will attend the training
workshops and relay this information to other workers at the different
dairies. Professional and technical expertise to develop the training work-
shops will be secured from such groups as CHIP and/or CNYCOSH.

GOAL # 3:

To assist United Auto Workers Local # 2300 with development of a comprehensive
program to deal with health and safety concerns of its' 900 members employed at
Cornell University. Because of the diverse tasks performed by the UAW members
the health and safety program will focus on 2 distinct occupational groupings.

A comprehensive survey of 140 laboratory attendants/custodians and 60 farmhands/
groundscrew will be conducted.

Objective # 1: To conduct a telephone/questionnaire survey of laboratory
zattendants/custodians and farmhands/groundscrew to identify health and
safety problems which are unique to their employment.

Objective # 2: To conduct at least four 2 hour group interviews with labora-
tory attendants/custodians in the Agricultural and Veterinary Schools to
identify health and safety problems associated with their employment. These
employees may have unique concerns, since their worksites are experimental
research laboratories. Research conducted in the laboratories, and to which
the employees are associated, include: animal diseases, plant mutation, gene-
tic research, and other clinical-type research.

Objective # 3: To conduct at least two 2 hour group interviews with farm-
hands/groundscrew from different worksites to clarify and identify health
and safety problems associated with their employment. These employees are

Objective # 6: To assist UAW Local # 2300 to establish a Health and Safety

C. Project. Outcome:

The Health and Safety Education Program is unique, since it is designed to
directly reach working men and women. The program will actively involve workers
in the identification of specific health and safety issues at diverse worksites
and the development of training sessions which are appropriate for each worksite.

Implementation of the proposed project will produce the following results:

4. Health and safety issues which are unique to the workers represented by
UAW Local 2300 and Teamsters Local 65 will be identified, with training
sessions based on these issues being provided to effected workers.

2. Through the activities of project staff, there will be an increase in the
understanding of health and safety issues within Tompkins and Cortland
counties. This will be accomplished by the development and implementation
of training sessions, publication and distribution of educational litera-
ture, attendance at local union general membership meetings, and community
presentations on health and safety.

3. Through the program design of involving workers in the development and im-
plementation of health and safety training there will be an increase in local
training programs. This will be facilitated by the attendance of project
staff at local union meetings and their assisting workers in development of
presentations to be given at the local meetings.

4. The project staff, to ensure participation in the health and safety train-
ing sessions, will contact the leadership of at least 50% of the local
unions within Tompkins and Cortland counties.
