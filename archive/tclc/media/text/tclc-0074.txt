FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
September 17, 1998

Contact: Greg Shaw

Workplace Ergonomic Evaluations Offered:

The Labor Coalition’s new staff ergono-
mist, Greg Shaw is available to perform no-
cost on-site ergonomic analyses to help work-
ers identify potentially harmful work envi-
ronments or work practices. If workers have
concerns about their work environments or
pain or discomfort they experience as a re-
sult of performing their job they should call
for a free consultation. The service is open
to all occupations and anyone concerned about
their health should feel free to call. Con-

tact Greg Shaw at the Labor Coalition in
Ithaca at (607) 277-5670.

The Labor Coalition’s health and safety
training and education program is in it’s
ninth year of operation with primary funding
from the New York State Department of Labor.

Greg Shaw is a professional ergonomist
with a Master’s degree from the Department of
Design and Environmental Analysis at Cornell
University. Before joining the Labor Coali-

tion he worked as an ergonomic consultant in
the Ithaca area.
