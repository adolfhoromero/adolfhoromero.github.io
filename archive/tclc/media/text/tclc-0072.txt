Local Labor Council Wins OSHA Grant

The Occupational Safety and Health Administration
(OSHA) has awarded a $469,000 grant to a national
consortium of labor-based safety and health
advocates, including Midstate Central Labor
Council (AFL-CIO). Midstate will receive $21,000
under the grant

The grant will fund 1998-99 implementation of the
"Protecting Workers Who Exercise Rights" (PWWER)
project. The PWWER project is designed to provide
workers with information, materials, training and
education aimed at increasing understanding of

accompany inspectors during an inspection; and to
be protected in cases where employers retaliate
against workers who exercise their rights under
the Occupational Safety and Health Act.

"It is outrageous that many workers work under
unsafe conditions because they either are unaware
of their rights to call for an OSHA inspection, or
because they are afraid of employer retaliation,"
said Mike Oates, President of the Midstate Labor
Council. "The educational materials and training

we will provide workers under this grant will help
remedy this problem."

In addition to producing a factsheet on workers’
rights under the Occupational Safety and Health
Act, Midstate CLC will conduct a series of
training programs in Tompkins, Cortland, Schuyler,
Steuben and Chemung Counties for workers,

including workers of color, teens and immigrant
workers.
