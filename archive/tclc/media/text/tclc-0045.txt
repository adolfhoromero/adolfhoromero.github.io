July 31, 1986

Robert Howard, Sheriff
Tompkins County

Dear Sheriff Howard,

As you areaware supporters of the Cargill strikers
(IAM Lodge 1607) have established a regular picketline
at the Cargill Salt facility. We are excercising our
lawful right to maintain a continuous moving picketline

on Portland Point Road. We will continue to do so until
the strike is settled,

We would like to have your assurance that all the
County deputies who are assigned to Portland Point Road
are familiar with our legal rights while picketing.
Furthermore, given the special circumstances of people
peacefully walking on the roadway, we would like to have
your directive that their safety will be protected. This
is also a duty of deputies on patrol there. The Cargill
Salt Co. has a specified speed limit of 5-10 miles per

hour on its raodways leading to Portland Point Road. We
would expect that deputies will direct traffic to maintain
that speed limit when approaching pedestrians.

Thank you for your cooperation in this matter.

Sincerely Yours,

Com Joga

Tom Joyce
Steering Committee

Cec, James Mason, Chair-Public Safe
