Midstate’s Busy Summer

The summer is always a busy time for
the Midstate Central Labor Council and this year
is no exception. We'll be celebrating our 20th
annual Labor Day Picnic this upcoming
September. Over the years the picnic has
developed into a major community event in our
area. The Picnic Committee— which is a subset
of the Labor Council— meets weekly throughout
the summer and needs your help. We want to
insure that this 20th anniversary is very special
and will need extra help in planning the event.
Please contact Linda Smith at 607 756-5582 for
more information and to get involved in the best
Labor Day celebration in the area...

This summer the Labor Council will also
be involved in the Dairy Parade in Cortland. We
need volunteers to help come up with creative
ideas for a float as well as outreach and
coordinating of events. Please contact Midge
Grant at 607 423-5563 for more information.

they approve of unions— a level not seen since

But in the face of this growing support, it
is also clear that the public has little knowledge of
employers’ aggressive attacks against workers
trying to exercise their freedom to form unions.
More than 75 percent of those surveyed say em-
ployers should take no position when workers
want to hold a union election, but only 44 percent
say they think employers oppose workers’ effort
to join unions.

In reality, nearly all employers use unfair
tactics to oppose workers’ freedom to form un-
ions, according to Kate Bronfenbrenner. The pub-
lic strongly disapproves of employer tactics to in-
timidate workers, such as requiring supervisors to
urge employees to vote against the union and
holding mandatory, closed-door anti-union meet-
ings— but even union members far underestimate
how often employers deploy these unscrupulous
methods. For example, only 20 percent of union
members surveyed thought employers require
workers to attend anti-union meetings, which they
do 92 percent of the time.

In February, the AFL-CIO Executive Council
passed a resolution to “educate, engage and mo-
bilize literally millions of union members and peo-
ple of conscience” to expose employers’ cam-
paign against workers and support state and local
legislation that supports organizing.
