The Labor Council is an affiliated and chartered union with the American ederation of Labor an

Congress of Industrial Organizations (AFL-CIO). The Labor Council is organized as an unincorporated
association and operated as a not-for-profit organization to support local member unions through

educational and informational programs. Membership in the Labor Council is open to affiliated local unions,
councils, joint boards, district councils, and other organizations supporting the goals of the Labor Council.

eae.

The Labor Council was placed into trusteeship by the President of the AFL-CIO on January 27, 1999.
The trusteeship was lifted in April, 1999.

Basis of Accounting é
The Labor Council's policy is to prepare its financial statements on the cash basis of accounting;

consequently, revenues are recognized when received rather than when earned, and expenses are
recognized when cash is disbursed rather than when the obligation is incurred.

Use of Estimates

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with generally accepted accounting principles
requires management to make estimates and assumptions that affect certain reported amounts and
disclosures. Accordingly, actual results could differ from those estimates.

Cash Equivalents
For purposes of the Statements of Cash Flows, the Labor Council considers all short-term instruments

purchased with a maturity of three months or less to be cash equivalents. There were no cash equivalents
at December 31, 2000 and 1999.

Tax Exemption
The Labor Council is exempt from income taxes under Section 501 (c)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Related Party Transactions

The Labor Council leased office space under an operating lease from a member, the UAW Local 2300,

for $387.50 per month through January, 2000. The Council receives $350.00 per month from the AFL-ClO
for use of a portion of the space.

Note 3 - Advertising

The Council uses advertising for various activities. The costs of advertising are expensed as incurred.
During 2000 and 1999, advertising costs totaled $201 and $613, respectively.
