Four issues of FYI, a Health and Safety Newsletter for Office

Workers were published. Topics covered included: repetitive strain

Workers Memorial Da
morxers Memorial Day

We held a number of activities to commemorate Workers Memorial
Day this year. Our memorial service and OSHA reform rally was
attended by 75 people, and included representatives from at least
fifteen local unions. The service was dedicated to the 25 Hamlet
fire victims. aA moment of silence was publicly observed at 2 pm
April 28 in Ithaca, Schuyler County and at SUNY-Cortland. In
Ithaca it was Signalled by the sounding of the City's fire horns
in the traditional "Death Bell," the Box 5-5-5-5 signal normally
used for the death of a firefighter. Four local jurisdictions -
the cities of Ithaca and Cortland, Schuyler County, and Tompkins
County - approved Workers Memorial Day proclamations.

Finally labor delegations met in Separate hour-long meetings
with the District Attorneys of Tompkins, Cortland and Schuyler
Counties, to press the issue of local criminal prosecution of
employers in cases in which their negligence or willful violation
of safety rules leads to a serious workplace injury, illness or
death.

groups of workers in non-unionized workplaces.

Information and Referral

During the course of the year we dealt with 132 unsolicited
requests for information, ranging from workers compensation issues
to concerns about specific chemicals in the workplace. This is a
73 percent increase in requests for information compared to the
previous year (76 requests dealt with). We also now have an
improved system in place for recording our activities in this area.

The Working Press continued to be used regularly as @
mechanism of community information and outreach; there were usually
two health and safety articles in each issue.
