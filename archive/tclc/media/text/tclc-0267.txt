CORNELL UNIVERSITY

New York State School of Industrial and Labor Relations, Extension Division

Presents:

WILL THE SIDE AGREEMENTS MAKE
NAFTA FAIR TO WORKING PEOPLE?

Trade unions, environmental groups, and citizen organizations have
organized against the proposed North American Free Trade Agree-
ment (NAFTA). These groups are concerned that passage of NAFTA
will result in the loss of hundreds of thousands of jobs and drive down
workers’ living standards. Numerous commentators have described
as "deplorable," the environmental mess created by North American
corporations in the "maquiladora zone," mostly located in Northern
Mexico.

The present Administration has negotiated "side agreements" to the
NAFTA with Mexico and Canada which it believes solves these
problems. This meeting will take a look at the "side agreements.”
We will discuss whether this "new NAFTA" should be supported by
trade unionists and working people.
