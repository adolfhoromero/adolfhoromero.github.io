THE TOMPKINS-CORTLAND LABOR COALITION

To all members of the Labor Coalition:

The next meeting will be on Thursday, March 28 at 6:30 PM
at 701 West State Street in Ithaca at the Plumbers and Pipefitters
Hall. This is a good meeting to attend as the Labor Movie of the
Month will follow directly at 7:30 PM. This months movie will be
"Harlan County, USA" the story of a bitter, year long strike in the
coal fields of Kentucky. We have heard that this is an excellant
film, both informative and moving. Bring a friend from your Local
and come and enjoy. February's movie, "The Inheritance" was well
attended and everyone got into the film as well as the discussion
that followed.

1. Plant Closing Legislation
2. Photo #xhibit
3. TC3 and Labor Coalition Workshop for H.S. students
Negotiation Update

| if you haven't already...and...send your photos to the Labor Coalition

. See you on the 28th.
