Dear Carl,

Although I’d conveyed my thanks to you verbally for loaning the
two videos on occupational health for my Sociology of Medicine
class, I want to repeat my appreciation in writing.

From the list you sent me, I chose one from CNYCOSH ("Hard Metals
Disease"--excellent and, because it includes Syracuse, highly
salient to our students), one from AFL-CIO ("Those Who Know Don’t
Tell"--very good capsule history of labor’s fight for
occupational health and safety. I showed it first and Le proved
a good lead-in to the other two.) and "A Price for Every
Progress: The Health Hazards of VDTs" Gets “zippy” style cauqhe
students’ attention, but more importantly, convinced them that
such questions are not merely the concern of miners or

machinists).
With your help, this segment of my course went very well--so much
so that I intend to expand it in the future.

Yours truly,

Craig B/ Little
Professor and Chair
Sociology/Anthropology
