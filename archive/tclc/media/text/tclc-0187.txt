STUDENT INTERNS

/ The Coalition is making a big effort to obtain some student interns from
Cornell's Industrial Labor Relations school for this summer and next
fall.

They will be available for the Coalition and your local. If
you have any reserach, Organizing, or legislative projects that need
a volunteer, contact Tom Joyce (272-0506) or Al Davidoff (272-4108).

PLANT CLOSINGS BILL

The mayor's committee comprised of 3 representatives from labor and

3 from business with a resource person for each group met on March 10
They heard a report from Steve Jackson, our resource person, who had

researched the effects of similar legislation around the country. It

was overwhelmingly positive and helped to debunk the major arguements

of the business community. The next meeting is April 7. The Coalition

hopes that the committee will wind down shortly afterwards and that
the legislation will go to Common Council.

We will have a brochure on the bill available soon.
