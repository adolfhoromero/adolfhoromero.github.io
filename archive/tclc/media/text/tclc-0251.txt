Office Work Is Dangerous to Our Health

by Carl Feuer, UUP and
Robin Whittlesey
Office work is dangerous to our health.

Did your know that:

* Female office workers experience
coronary heart disease about twice as often
as other working women.

* In a study of 22,000 workers in 130
occupations, the government found that
secretaries suffered the second highest
rate of stress-related disease of all 130
occupations.

* Female office workers who spend at least
20 hours a week working at computers
have double the normal rate of
miscarriages in the first trimester.

* Carpal tunnel syndrome and eyestrain
are increasingly common occupational
diseases among office workers.

* Recent health surveys have documented
that office workers suffer a variety of
ailments due to poor office air quality
ranging from drowsiness and decreased
alertness to allergic attacks.
