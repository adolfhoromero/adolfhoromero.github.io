Chuck,

A couple of thoughts to throw into the Directions Committee deliberations.

This is a little off the cuff but presumedly it can be refined in later dis-
cussions, if need be.

1, Meetings - It sounds as if there was a good discussion already and

perhaps a move in that direction. The one clear problem that I have noticed

is that meetings are often canceled, or put off to another date, The. sense

that “there wasn't enough on the agenda" or various other reasons just haven't
cut it for me. If we're going to be serious as an organiaation, let's meet
when we say we're going to meet. I can't imagine a time when there arn't

issues to discuss or decide upon. It doesn't take long for a sense that a

small group of people is making all the decisions and this then reinforces

some of the problems that others have already raised. So, as others have

pointed out, regular, perhaps more often, meetings with an attempt to make
them more meaningful can build a more regular participation.

2. "Issue" focus - I have thought for some time that it would make

sense for the Labor Coalition to focus on one area. I hesitiate to use the
word "issue" because I am not one to promote one issue organizing. However,

I can see a great advantage and utility to pressing limited resources in one
area. We have done a great job of working into the health and safety area.
We're well grounded in that, have tremendous resources, are known to be
expert, etc. And, despite current financial insecurities, we will maintain
that as a Labor Coalition focus. It seems to me it is time to try that cane :
