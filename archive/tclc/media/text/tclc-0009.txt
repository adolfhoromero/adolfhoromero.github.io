Dear Brother Bonthius:
Welcome to the New York State AFL-CIO!

We are pleased that your labor council has decided to affiliate with
the state federation. Your participation will go along way in
strengthening the voice of organized labor in New York State.

We have placed you on our mailing list for our regular publications
and notices. We hope you will be able to participate in the many
activities sponsored by the state AFL-CIO. We have a number of new and
exciting programs but they are only effective if our local affiliates take
advantage of them.

If we can be of any assistance, please do not hesitate to call us.

Again, welcome to the state AFL-CIO. We're glad to have you aboard.
