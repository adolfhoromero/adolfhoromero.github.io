RESOLUTION

We the Board of Supervisors of the City of Cortland in the
State of New York hereby agree that:

1. We publicly denounce Smith Corona's decision to move it's
operations from the Cortland area to Mexico without consideration
of the impact to it's workers and the communities to which they are
eepart; and

2. We support the workers of the Smith Corona pliant located
in the Town of Cortlandville and who live mainly in and around our
city in their efforts to receive a fair and equitable settlement
form their employer, Smith Corona Merchant. We also pledge to
support them in their just grievances which they may have with
Smith Corona over matters dealing with either medical or financial
hardships that may surface now or in the future; and

3. We request form Smith Corona that they provide some sort
of financial compensation and/or assistance for the community that
has supported and assisted in its growth throughout the last
eighty-three (83) years, and is now left with the aftermath of a
disaster of as yet undetermined magnitude; and
