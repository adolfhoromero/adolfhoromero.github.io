with UAW Local 2300
December 21, 1998

1) Rental Arrangement, as of May 1, 1997

MCLC rents office space, including two rooms, access to the meeting room,
heat, electricity, etc. from UAW 2300.

One of the rooms is used solely by the health/safety trainers; the other room
is used approximately 25 percent by the Labor Coalition’s Coordinator (part-
time; she also does a considerable amount of work from her home) and
officers. The rest of the time the room is used by the AFL-CIO, specifically
their NYS Director, who lives in Ithaca. The AFL-CIO provides a monthly
subsidy to MCLC, which includes an amount to cover use of the room.

2) Copier payments: Per longstanding verbal agreement, MCLC reimburses
UAW Local 2300 for one-half of the copier rental bill, which includes
monthly lease payment and cost per copy.

3) Payments for copier paper: Per longstanding verbal agreement, MCLC
reimburses UAW Local 2300 for one-half of the copier paper bill, as
itemized by receipts from T.G. Miller’s Sons Paper Co. and other vendors.

4) Payments for postage meter use: Per longstanding verbal agreement,
MCL-C alternates “refilling” the postage meter (usually $300.00 at a time)

with UAW Local 2300. At this time, MCLC does not contribute toward the

meter rent or Stal scale rent pai Pitney-Bow AW
Local 2300.
