Pages

The Working Press was published by the Tompkins-Cortland Labor Coalition, 109 West State Street, Ithaca, New York. Pages were scanned at 1200 dpi. The page text is read from the scans (OCR) and can contain errors; the page images remain the authoritative record.

Rights & corrections. This archive is offered for research and education. If you hold rights in this material, or you can help identify a person, local, or event on any page, please get in touch. Corrections and additions are welcome.
